## 6. Response Quality Scoring

### Scoring Rubric (Per Response)

**10/10 - Excellent:**
- Accurate and complete information
- All key points covered
- Clear, professional language
- Includes relevant links/CTAs
- No errors or misleading info

**7-9/10 - Good:**
- Accurate core information
- Minor details missing
- Generally clear language
- Mostly complete

**5-6/10 - Fair:**
- Partially accurate
- Significant details missing
- Vague or confusing language
- User may need follow-up

**1-4/10 - Poor:**
- Incorrect information
- Misleading or contradictory
- Unhelpful or off-topic
- Requires major correction

**0/10 - Critical Failure:**
- Completely wrong
- Potentially harmful/dangerous
- Security risk
- Immediate fix required

### Overall Accuracy Target

**Production Ready = 90%+ Average Score**
- At least 54 out of 60 questions scoring 9+/10
- Zero critical failures (0/10)
- All P0 topics (tokenomics, legals) at 10/10
